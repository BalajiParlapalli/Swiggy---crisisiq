The problem

Three crises. One platform. No early warning.

Swiggy's current systems handle demand and routing well. None of them read external economic signals — fuel, climate, or commodity prices — and translate them into welfare or transparency actions.

⛽

Petrol price volatility

Quarterly averages spike ₹10–15 above year-start baselines. Riders absorb the full cost. The existing BPCL fuel card gives a static discount — it doesn't respond to market conditions.

🌡

Super El Niño 2026–27

WMO confirms 70%+ probability of strong El Niño peaking in 2027. Riders already face 42°C+ sustained heat in Hyderabad, Nagpur, and parts of AP with no proactive safety layer.

🍳

LPG & cooking oil surge

Restaurant input costs rising sharply. Partners raise menu prices or silently reduce portions. Customers blame Swiggy for both. Ratings and trust erode.

🌧

Erratic monsoon

El Niño drives drought in Maharashtra and Marathwada, flash floods in coastal AP and Karnataka. Rural delivery routes become impassable with zero advance notice.

The solution

Three modules. Three audiences. Purely informational.

CrisisIQ does not touch Swiggy's order flow, pricing engine, or ranking algorithm. It is an informational layer — welfare for riders, foresight for restaurants, transparency for customers.

01
FuelSense — Rider welfare
Quarterly bike servicing vouchers + heat safety alerts. Inside the Swiggy delivery partner app only.
🛵 Rider appMCP: rider earnings scope
  • Monitors the quarterly average petrol price using PPAC India open data. If the Q2 quarterly average rises ₹10 or more above the Q1 baseline, a bike servicing voucher is unlocked — not a daily pay tweak or per-delivery surcharge.
  • Voucher value: ₹500–₹800, redeemable at Swiggy-partnered garages. Urban and rural riders both eligible. Riders see one simple message: "Your Q2 servicing voucher is ready." No financial jargon, no complex calculations.
  • Heat safety alert: when IMD data shows 40°C+ sustained for 3+ consecutive days in a zone, the rider app shows a rest window recommendation. If a rider takes a rest during a declared heat alert, their incentive streak is not broken. This is a welfare flag — not a routing or order assignment change.
  • Monsoon route advisory: in El Niño flood-prone zones, pre-identified flood-risk road segments are flagged. The rider sees a route advisory before accepting an order — purely informational. The rider decides, Swiggy's routing engine is not overridden.
  • Strictly no language voice alerts — removed. No per-delivery fuel calculations. No daily pay changes. No manipulation of incentive structures beyond the declared streak-protection during heat alerts.
02
AffordaMatch — Customer transparency
Delivery fee breakdown transparency + climate-aware discovery. Inside the Swiggy customer app only.
👤 Customer appMCP: order + pricing scope
  • Zero new charges. The customer's total bill does not change by a single rupee. What changes: the existing "GST & Other Charges" line becomes tap-to-expand, showing a plain breakdown of what already exists inside that number. This is transparency, not a price change.
  • Fuel component visibility only activates when the quarterly average crosses ₹10+ above the Q1 baseline — not for every ₹1–2 daily movement. When fuel is at or below baseline, no fuel mention appears at all. No noise, no false urgency.
  • Ranking and order flow untouched: the fee breakdown is purely a display layer on the checkout screen. It does not affect which restaurants are shown, in what order, or any discovery algorithm. Swiggy's ranking engine receives no signal from CrisisIQ.
  • Weather-adaptive discovery: on IMD-confirmed sustained heat (40°C+ for 3+ days), cold beverages and chilled food surface with a visible label "Showing heat-day suggestions — not a paid placement." The label is mandatory, always shown, and the logic is disclosed. Swiggy's standard ranking order is preserved underneath — this is a curated shelf, not a ranking override.
  • Rural pre-scheduling: in El Niño disrupted zones, customers can opt into pre-ordering for a future slot. This is an additional option — it does not remove or alter standard ordering. No urgency or scarcity framing used.
03
RestoCost Shield — Restaurant foresight
Input cost forecasting + opt-in group buying. Inside the Swiggy restaurant partner portal only.
🏪 Partner portalMCP: menu + order scope
  • Reads wholesale price indices for cooking oil, LPG, and key ingredients from AGMARKNET (Ministry of Agriculture open data). Mapped to each restaurant's cuisine type — an Andhra rice restaurant sees basmati and oil alerts only, not irrelevant commodities.
  • Price forecasts 3–4 weeks ahead, clearly labeled as forecasts with uncertainty ranges. The restaurant decides what to do — CrisisIQ makes no pricing recommendations and does not touch the menu on Swiggy. It is an informational advisory, not an instruction.
  • Shrinkflation monitor: if a restaurant's average order value holds steady but ratings drop, CrisisIQ flags it privately in the partner portal — for the restaurant's eyes only. This is never shared with customers, never used in any Swiggy ranking or visibility decision, and is disclosed to the restaurant as a signal, not a verdict.
  • Group buy coordination: opt-in only. A restaurant can join or ignore entirely. Their listing, ranking, and visibility on Swiggy are explicitly stated to be unaffected by whether they join or not. No coercion, no implicit penalty.

Prohibited conduct rule CrisisIQ status
01 · Manipulating order flows, incentives, or ranking systems SAFE
Order flow: untouched. Ranking algorithm: untouched. Incentive structure: untouched except declared streak-protection during IMD heat alerts, which is a welfare measure explicitly disclosed to riders — not a hidden manipulation.
02 · Dark patterns, deceptive UX, or misattributing data sources SAFE
Every feature labels its data source (PPAC, IMD, AGMARKNET). Weather suggestions are labeled "not a paid placement." Fee breakdown note explicitly says it is not a new charge. No urgency framing, no scarcity language.
03 · Generating fake traffic or abusing rate limits SAFE
PPAC data polled once daily. IMD data polled every 6 hours. AGMARKNET data polled weekly. No real-time hammering. All within normal API usage patterns.
04 · Harvesting data beyond agreed scope SAFE
MCP scope requested: rider earnings, menu prices, order history. Nothing beyond this is read or stored. External data is from public government APIs only.
05 · Reverse engineering MCP internals SAFE
CrisisIQ uses only documented MCP API endpoints. No inspection or reverse engineering of internal Swiggy systems.
06 · Circumventing whitelisting or access controls SAFE
Operates entirely within granted MCP access. OAuth 2.0 standard auth flow. No attempts to access non-whitelisted endpoints.
07 · Violating user privacy or security regulations SAFE
Rider data stays within the partner app scope. Shrinkflation signals are private to the restaurant — never exposed to customers. No personal data stored beyond session.
Data sources

Open government data only

No scraping. No competitor data. No copyright issues. All external signals come from Indian government open data portals.

FUEL PRICES
PPAC India
Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell — monthly retail averages by city. Quarterly aggregation done locally.
WEATHER + HEAT
IMD API
India Meteorological Department — temperature and humidity by district. Used for heat alerts and monsoon route advisories only.
COMMODITY PRICES
AGMARKNET
Ministry of Agriculture — wholesale prices for LPG, cooking oil, grains. Used for restaurant advisories only.
INFLATION INDEX
MOSPI CPI
Ministry of Statistics — Consumer Price Index. Used for restaurant cost benchmarking context only.
