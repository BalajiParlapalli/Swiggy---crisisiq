The problem

Three crises converging on delivery economics

By mid-2026, Swiggy faces simultaneous shocks to fuel costs, food ingredient prices, and rider safety from extreme heat — none of which its current systems address proactively.

⛽
Petrol price volatility
Global supply shocks are pushing fuel costs up. Riders absorb this directly. Swiggy's existing BPCL fuel card is static — it doesn't adjust to real-time price spikes by PIN code or route distance.
🌡
Super El Niño 2026–27
WMO confirms 70%+ probability of a strong El Niño peaking in 2027. Heatwaves are already record-breaking. Riders face health crises in 42°C+ zones with no proactive system — only reactive cooling vests.
🍳
LPG & cooking oil surge
Restaurant input costs are rising sharply. Partners raise prices or silently reduce portions. Customers leave bad reviews not understanding why. Swiggy's margins and ratings both suffer.
🌧
Erratic monsoon disruption
El Niño brings drought in some zones, flash floods in others — particularly Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra. Rural delivery routes become impassable with zero advance warning in the current system.
The solution

Three linked modules. One AI engine.

CrisisIQ reads PPAC fuel prices, IMD climate data, and AGMARKNET commodity indices — all open government data — and surfaces decisions for riders, restaurants, and customers through Swiggy's MCP APIs.

01
FuelSense
Dynamic fuel-parity pay + climate safety for riders
🏘 Rural 🏙 Urban MCP: rider earnings
  • Reads live petrol prices by PIN code from PPAC government API. When fuel crosses a threshold, automatically adds a per-km fuel-parity top-up to rider payout — shown as a flame icon, not financial jargon.
  • Tracks ethanol-blend fuel (E20), cheaper in many rural areas. Riders using E20 bikes get a calibrated top-up rate — not the same flat rate as petrol riders. Prevents rural underpayment.
  • Heat index alert: when IMD temperature + humidity crosses a danger threshold, the app shows a rest-reminder and unlocks "heat break" status — rider pauses 15 min without losing incentive streak. Prevents heat stroke before it happens.
  • Monsoon route intelligence: in El Niño flood-prone zones (Andhra, Karnataka coasts), marks road segments impassable in real time. In drought zones where order volume drops, shows next predicted high-demand window.
  • Literacy-first UI: all alerts use colour (green/yellow/red), large icons, and a 5-second voice clip in local language (Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Kannada, Bengali). Zero paragraph text in the rider alert layer.
02
RestoCost Shield
Restaurant input cost intelligence + group buying
🏙 Urban 🏘 Tier-2 towns MCP: restaurant menu data
  • Aggregates wholesale price indices for cooking oil, LPG, and key ingredients from AGMARKNET (open data), mapped to each restaurant's primary cuisine type. A biryani place gets alerts about basmati + oil only.
  • Predicts price increases 2–4 weeks ahead using El Niño crop impact forecasts + commodity trend data. Restaurant gets a gentle nudge: "Cooking oil likely +18% in 3 weeks — consider a ₹15 buffer on rice dishes."
  • Portion-shrinkflation detector: if a restaurant's average order price is stable but customer ratings are dropping, the AI flags likely portion-reduction — preventing the silent quality decay that destroys long-term trust.
  • Group buy coordination: clusters nearby restaurant partners to unlock wholesale LPG and cooking oil pricing together. No existing delivery platform in India does this for restaurant partners. Inspired by BigBasket's B2B model, repurposed for food partners.
03
AffordaMatch
Customer price transparency + climate-aware discovery
🏘 Tier-2/3 cities 🏙 Cost-aware urban MCP: order + pricing data
  • Fuel-adjusted delivery fee transparency: instead of a mysterious delivery charge, shows "Today's delivery fee includes ₹4 fuel cost." When fuel drops, shows "Fuel savings today: ₹3 off delivery." Customers understand volatility, stop blaming Swiggy for fee changes.
  • "Steady Price" restaurant badge: restaurants whose prices haven't inflated beyond the local CPI rate get a visible badge. The logic is disclosed to users — no dark pattern, no ranking manipulation. A transparent utility feature.
  • Weather-adaptive suggestions: on a 44°C day, surfaces cold beverages and cold meals as a utility feature — not a paid placement. On a monsoon day, surfaces comfort food. Behaviorally relevant, not manipulative.
  • Rural pre-scheduling: in areas with erratic road access from El Niño disruptions, lets customers pre-order for a future slot. Riders plan routes in advance. Restaurants prepare batch. Reduces no-delivery failures significantly in disrupted zones.
